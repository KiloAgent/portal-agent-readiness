# Portal check

H1: Could an AI agent work in your portal?

Sub: Tell us about one portal you log into every week. Get a one-page readiness report: the best route in, the risks, and a message you can send the vendor.

## Form

- Portal name (required)
- Portal URL (optional, stored only, not fetched)
- What you do there (task)
- How often (daily, weekly, monthly, ad hoc)
- Minutes per run
- Login method
- Data out
- Data in
- Terms for automation
- How often the UI changes
- I have a contact at the vendor
- Email
- Newsletter checkbox, left unticked
- Button: Get my readiness report

Under the button: One email with a link to your report. No call, no booking.

## After submit

Show tier, route, and a one-line summary on screen.

Example: Your portal: Tier B, Ready with a workaround. Route: export and email.

The full report (steps, risks, vendor message) arrives by emailed link.

## Footer

Imprint, Privacy link, ZenStudy Technologies FZCO, Technohub 1, Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai, UAE.

## Do not

- No Book or Cal link
- No pricing
- No retention line
- No live fetch of the portal URL
